Privacy Policy for MJ AUTOS

Last updated: November 2026

MJ AUTOS ("we", "our", or "us") operates the MJ AUTOS mobile application and Point of Sale (POS) inventory management service. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Application.

1. Information We Collect

Because MJ AUTOS is an internal business management and billing application for staff and shop owners:

  • Account Credentials: We store authorized employee and owner login credentials (email and securely encrypted passwords) to authenticate access.
  • Business & Transaction Data: Invoices, billing records, customer names (optional for receipts), and stock inventory records created by users.
  • Device & Media Permissions: If you use receipt printing or upload product/expense documents, the app may request camera or storage access solely for performing those business functions.

2. Use of Information

The information collected is used exclusively for:

  • Authenticating staff and maintaining secure role-based sessions.
  • Generating, maintaining, and printing business invoices and stock reports.
  • Internal business analytics and expense tracking for the shop owner.

3. Data Protection and Security

We take the security of your business and personal data seriously. All communications between the app and server are encrypted using HTTPS/SSL. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using industry-standard algorithms, and session tokens are protected via secure HTTP cookies.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal or business data to third-party advertising companies or data brokers. Data is stored solely on private, secure cloud infrastructure.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the MJ AUTOS administrative team directly.
